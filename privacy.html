CarLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · App: https://mycarlog.github.io/

CarLog is a free, personal car log. It keeps track of fuel fill-ups, payments, and service reminders for your own vehicles. This page explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Information you enter

Cars, fill-ups, payments, payers, and service records that you type into CarLog are stored on your own device, in your browser's local storage. The developer does not run a server for CarLog and cannot see this data.

Sign in with Google and Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", CarLog asks for one permission only: access to its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). CarLog saves a single backup file there (carlog-backup.json) so that you can restore your data or open it on another device with the same Google account.

  • CarLog cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address is shown inside the app only so that you can see which account is connected.
  • Your data goes directly between your device and your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server.

Photos you scan

When you use "Scan odometer" or "Scan bill", the photo is read on your device. It is not uploaded, saved, or shared by CarLog.

Services used by the app

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files.
  • Google provides sign-in and Google Drive, and Google Fonts for the app's typefaces.
  • Public code libraries (for example jsDelivr for the on-device text reader, and SheetJS for Excel export) may be downloaded when you first use those features.

These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their files, under their own privacy policies.

What CarLog does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • It does not use cookies, apart from your browser's local storage for the app's own data and settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CarLog's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: in CarLog open Settings → Clear / Reset Data, or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • In Google Drive: remove CarLog's app data under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
